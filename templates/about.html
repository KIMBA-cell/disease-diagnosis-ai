📌 About The System

The AI Disease Diagnosis System is an intelligent web-based application designed to predict possible diseases based on symptoms selected by the user. It uses Machine Learning algorithms trained on a medical dataset of thousands of records to provide accurate disease predictions instantly. This system is built as a Final Year Project to demonstrate the power of Artificial Intelligence in the healthcare sector.

📖 How To Use
  • 1
    Register for an account or log in if you already have one
  • 2
    Select Yes for any symptom you are currently experiencing
  • 3
    Click the Predict Disease button
  • 4
    The AI will analyze your symptoms and display the predicted disease
  • 5
    Read the disease description, severity level, and recommended precautions carefully
  • 6
    Always consult a qualified medical doctor for proper diagnosis
